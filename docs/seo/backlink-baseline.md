## 2. Strict Quality & Anti-Spam Policy

FolkMiles maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against manipulative or black-hat link building:

- ❌ **NO Paid Link Farms or PBNs**: Algorithmic updates (Google Penguin / SpamBrain) identify and devalue artificial link networks, risking manual actions or algorithmic suppression.
- ❌ **NO Automated Comment Spam or Forum Blasts**: Degrades brand trust and triggers Google Search spam filters.
- ❌ **NO Link Exchange Wheels**: "I link to you, you link to me" networks violate Google Webmaster Guidelines.
- ❌ **NO Low-Quality Directory Blasts**: Submissions are restricted strictly to legitimate, human-reviewed business directories (TripAdvisor, Justdial, Bing Places, IndiaMART).

## 3. White-Hat Organic Acquisition Strategy

1. **Foundational Business Citations**:
   - NAP consistency across top 10 Indian and global directories (TripAdvisor, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Justdial).
2. **Editorial Digital PR**:
   - Pitching genuine, unique story angles on Rajasthan's living culture, desert conservation, and unhurried travel to high-authority publications.
3. **Curated Blogger & Creator Collaborations**:
   - Partnering with authentic Indian travel writers and photographers seeking genuine local insights rather than commercialized tourist traps.
4. **Authoritative Resource Mentions**:
   - Earning citations on university cultural research pages, wildlife portals (e.g. Great Indian Bustard conservation in Desert National Park), and regional heritage guides.
