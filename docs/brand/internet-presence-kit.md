## 2. Standard Business Descriptions

### Short Description (approx. 150 characters)
FolkMiles is an India travel company offering private, locally guided journeys and authentic cultural experiences, beginning in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan.

### Medium Description (approx. 300 characters)
FolkMiles brings travellers into genuine connection with India through the eyes of locals. From living fort walks and merchant havelis to secluded desert camps and stargazing in the Thar Desert, our private journeys are shaped with transparent planning and authentic local knowledge.

### Full Description (approx. 700 characters)
FolkMiles is an India-focused travel company and tour operator built around the philosophy "Explore by Locals." We believe the most memorable travel experiences come from people who know a destination beyond the tourist map. 

Our active operational chapter begins in the Golden City of Jaisalmer, Rajasthan. We curate private, chauffeured journeys ranging from 2 to 4 days, featuring boutique heritage stays, sunset camel treks on quiet sand dunes, authentic Kalbelia folk performances, and respectful village artisan walks. 

With dedicated WhatsApp support, seasoned local drivers, and clear written quotations, FolkMiles provides unhurried, personalized travel across Rajasthan, with a vision for nationwide expansion.
