## 1. Google Review Direct Link Architecture

When the Google Business Profile is verified and published:
1. Access the Google Business Profile Manager (`business.google.com`).
2. Click **"Ask for reviews"** button on the home dashboard.
3. Copy the official direct review short URL: `https://g.page/r/[CID_OR_HASH]/review` or `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[PLACE_ID]`.
4. Create a redirect URL on `folkmiles.com` (e.g. `https://folkmiles.com/review`) for easy verbal sharing, printed collateral, and QR codes.

## 2. Review Acquisition Protocol & Ethics

### Strict Compliance Rules
- **No Incentives**: NEVER offer discounts, gifts, cash, or freebies in exchange for reviews. (Violates Google Anti-Incentivization Policies and Indian Consumer Protection Act guidelines).
- **No Fake Reviews**: Zero tolerance for fabricated reviews or review circles. Every review must stem from real paying travellers or bona fide inquiries.
- **No In-Camp Review Gatekeeping**: Do NOT stand over guests or demand reviews while they are checking out of the camp or cab.
- **Pacing / Natural Velocity**: Collect 2–4 reviews per week naturally as guests conclude their trips. Sudden spikes of 50 reviews in 24 hours trigger automated algorithmic spam filters.

### Optimal Timing
- **The Golden Window**: 2 to 6 hours after final drop-off at Jaisalmer Railway Station / Jodhpur Airport, or 10:00 AM the morning after return home.
- **Why**: The guest has completed their journey, is reflecting on the memories, and has internet connectivity on the train or flight home.
